Brings the features of the popular Chrome trade extensions (Better Trading, TradeUX) to the [Path of Exile 2 trade site](https://www.pathofexile.com/trade2) in every browser, Firefox first. Made for Violentmonkey, Tampermonkey and Greasemonkey 4 should work too.

![The trade site with quick filters and the bookmarks sidebar](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maluramichael/poe2-trade-monkey/master/docs/screenshots/overview.png)

## Features

Every feature can be switched off in the settings.

**Bookmarks that survive league changes**
- Folders with currency or ascendancy icons, drag and drop sorting, archive.
- Searches open in the league you are playing now, so your folders work again in every new league.
- "Check in current league" warns when a saved search uses stats that no longer exist.
- Share folders as codes, import Better Trading codes and backups.

![Bookmark menu](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maluramichael/poe2-trade-monkey/master/docs/screenshots/bookmark-menu.png)

**History and pins**
- The last 50 searches, reopen them in their league or in the current one.
- Pin results to compare them, one click brings you back to the search they came from.
- The browser tab shows the bookmark name or the searched item.

![A pinned item next to the result list](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maluramichael/poe2-trade-monkey/master/docs/screenshots/pins.png)

**Results**
- Mods that match your stat filters are highlighted.
- "+" and "−" next to every mod add it as a filter or exclude it.
- Identical listings from the same seller at the same price are grouped.
- Price equivalent in Divine and Exalted Orbs from poe.ninja.

**Search form**
- Two-column layout: filters left, results right, search buttons stay at the bottom.
- Quick filters: corrupted, fractured, desecrated, item level, quality, rarity and more.
- Always fuzzy search: the item and stat search fields start with ~.
- Star stats to keep them on top of the stat dropdown.

![Quick filter bar above Search and Clear](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/maluramichael/poe2-trade-monkey/master/docs/screenshots/quick-filters.png)

## Fair to the trade site

The script only enhances what the site loads anyway. It never sends searches on its own, never polls the trade API and never clicks whisper or "Travel to Hideout" for you.
